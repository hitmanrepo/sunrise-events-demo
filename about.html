Our story

Back in 2009, Raj started Sunrise Events from a small corner of Dighori: just a handful of balloons, a scooter, and a passion for setting up decorations door to door. At the time, no one imagined how far it would grow.

Over 16+ years, we've gone from "the balloon people" to a full birthday and event planning team, from haldi ceremonies to godbharai, terrace parties to engagement stages. Today we have our own team of trained decorators, our own material, and a single goal: making every celebration unforgettable.

7,550+ events, 14,000+ customers. These aren't just numbers to us, they're relationships. Behind every setup is a family's happiness, and that's what gets us up early every morning.
