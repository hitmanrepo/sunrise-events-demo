Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We can often manage same-day decoration if a slot is available, but for the best results and full design customization, it's better to book 1-2 days ahead by sending a photo on WhatsApp. Weekend and festival dates fill up quickly.

Which areas in Nagpur do you cover?

We set up across all of Nagpur: Dighori, Manewada, Besa, Hudkeshwar, Wardha Road, Sitabuldi, Dharampeth, Sadar, Wathoda, Koradi and the surrounding areas. Send us your address on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the size of the package: smaller room decorations are done in 1-1.5 hours, while bigger terrace or canopy setups can take 2-3 hours. We arrive before your event time so everything is ready in advance.

Can I choose my own theme or colours?

Absolutely! Our packages are just a starting point. Send us any reference photo on WhatsApp and we'll customise the colours, balloon style or props to match, with no extra hassle.

How does payment work?

Cash, UPI and bank transfer are all available. An advance may be needed for bigger bookings (like weddings or engagement stages), while smaller packages are typically paid for after setup.

What if I only need balloons or a small add-on?

No problem. Smaller add-ons like a balloon bouquet, cold pyro entry, or car boot surprise can be booked separately. Check the 'Surprises & Add-ons' category or just ask us directly on WhatsApp.
