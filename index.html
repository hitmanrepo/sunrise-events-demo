Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Kitne time pehle booking karni chahiye?

Hum same-day decoration bhi manage kar dete hain agar slot available ho, lekin best results aur design customization ke liye 1-2 din pehle WhatsApp par photo bhejkar book karna better hai. Weekend aur festival dates jaldi bhar jaati hain.

Aap Nagpur mein kaunse areas cover karte ho?

Hum poore Nagpur mein setup karte hain — Dighori, Manewada, Besa, Hudkeshwar, Wardha Road, Sitabuldi, Dharampeth, Sadar, Wathoda, Koradi aur aas-paas ke saare areas. Address WhatsApp par bhejiye, hum confirm kar denge.

Setup mein kitna time lagta hai?

Package ke size par depend karta hai — chhote room decorations 1-1.5 hours mein ho jaate hain, jabki bade terrace ya canopy setups 2-3 hours le sakte hain. Hum aapke event time se pehle pahunch kar poora setup taiyaar rakhte hain.

Kya main apna khud ka theme ya colour choose kar sakta hoon?

Bilkul! Hamare packages ek starting point hain — aap koi bhi reference photo WhatsApp par bhejiye aur hum colours, balloons ka style ya props usi hisaab se customize kar denge, bina extra jhanjhat ke.

Payment kaise hoti hai?

Cash, UPI aur bank transfer — teeno options available hain. Advance ki zarurat bade bookings (jaise wedding ya engagement stage) ke liye ho sakti hai, baaki chhote packages mein setup ke baad hi payment le lete hain.

Agar mujhe sirf balloons ya ek chhota add-on chahiye toh?

Koi baat nahi — balloon bouquet, cold pyro entry, car boot surprise jaise chhote add-ons bhi alag se book kar sakte hain. 'Surprises & Add-ons' category dekhiye ya seedha WhatsApp par pooch lijiye.
